Lumen and Loft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Lumen and Loft respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information we may collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights available to you. It applies when you visit our website, contact us, or use our interior design services in England.

  1. Who we are

Lumen and Loft is the organisation responsible for handling your personal information under this policy. If you have questions or want to exercise your rights, contact us using the contact details provided on our website or in our communications with you.

  1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

• Contact and identity details, such as your name, email address, telephone number and postal address. • Project information, such as your design preferences, property details, measurements, photographs, plans, budget and project requirements. • Communications and enquiry details, including information you provide when you contact us or request a consultation or quotation. • Transaction and service information relating to quotes, invoices, payments and work we provide. • Website and technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information and how you use our website. Cookies or similar technologies may collect some of this information, subject to your preferences and applicable law.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry or project.

  1. How and why we use your information

We may use your information to:

• Respond to enquiries and provide consultations, quotations and interior design services. • Plan, manage and deliver projects, including communicating with you and coordinating necessary project activities. • Manage payments, accounts and business records. • Maintain, secure and improve our website and services. • Send marketing communications where permitted by law. You can opt out at any time. • Meet legal obligations, protect our rights and prevent or address fraud or misuse.

  1. Our legal bases for processing

Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases, as applicable:

• Contract: processing needed to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to perform a contract with you. • Legitimate interests: processing needed for our legitimate business interests, such as responding to enquiries, managing our services and improving our website, where those interests are not overridden by your rights. • Legal obligation: processing needed to comply with the law. • Consent: processing based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew consent.

  1. Sharing your information

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our business, such as website hosting, IT, payment, communication, accounting or project-support providers. They may use the information only to provide services to us and must protect it appropriately.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell your personal information.

  1. International transfers

If a service provider processes your information outside the United Kingdom, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place.

  1. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. The appropriate period depends on the type of information and the circumstances. When it is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it where practicable.

  1. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to make it work, remember preferences and understand how visitors use it. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, where available, our cookie preference tool. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

  1. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, have inaccurate information corrected, request erasure or restriction, object to certain processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details described in section 1. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns first.

  1. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  1. Children's information

Our services and website are not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information without appropriate authorisation. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

  1. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on our website with its updated date.
